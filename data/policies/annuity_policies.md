# Harborview SecureGrowth Fixed Annuity: Contract Summary

## 1. Eligibility and Premiums
- Issue ages: 18 to 85.
- Minimum initial premium: $10,000.
- Additional premiums: minimum $500 each.
- Total premiums above $1,000,000 require written approval from Harborview.

## 2. Free Look Period
You may cancel within 20 days of receiving the contract and get a full refund of your premium.

## 3. Interest Rates
- Initial interest rate: 4.25% per year, guaranteed for the first 5 contract years.
- After year 5, Harborview declares a renewal rate each year.
- The guaranteed minimum interest rate is 1.50% per year at all times.
- This annuity does not use a market value adjustment.

## 4. Surrender Charges
If you withdraw more than the free amount, a surrender charge applies based on the contract year:

| Contract Year | Surrender Charge |
|---|---|
| 1 | 8% |
| 2 | 7% |
| 3 | 6% |
| 4 | 5% |
| 5 | 4% |
| 6 | 3% |
| 7 | 2% |
| 8 and later | 0% |

## 5. Free Withdrawals
Starting in contract year 2, you may withdraw up to 10% of your account value each year with no surrender charge. No free withdrawals are available in contract year 1.

## 6. Nursing Home Waiver
Surrender charges are waived if the owner is confined to a licensed nursing facility for 90 consecutive days. The confinement must begin after the first contract year.

## 7. Death Benefit
If the owner dies before annuitization, the beneficiary receives the greater of (a) the current account value or (b) total premiums paid minus withdrawals. Harborview pays within 30 days of receiving proof of death.

## 8. Annuitization (Income Payments)
- Income payments may begin no earlier than contract year 5 and no later than age 95.
- Payout options: Life Only, Life with 10-Year Period Certain, and Joint and Survivor.

## 9. Fees
- There is no annual contract fee.
- Expedited wire withdrawals cost $25 each.
- Standard check or ACH withdrawals are free.

## 10. Taxes
Harborview does not give tax advice. Withdrawals before age 59½ may be subject to an additional IRS penalty. Please consult a qualified tax professional.
