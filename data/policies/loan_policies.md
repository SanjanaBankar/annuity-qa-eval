# Harborview Personal Loan: Terms and Policy

## 1. Eligibility
- Applicant must be at least 18 years old and a U.S. resident.
- Minimum credit score: 620.
- Maximum debt-to-income (DTI) ratio: 45%.
- Proof of income is required.
- Co-signers are allowed.

## 2. Loan Amounts and Terms
- Loan amount: $2,000 to $40,000.
- Repayment terms: 12 to 84 months.
- APR range: 7.99% to 24.99%, depending on credit profile.
- Autopay discount: 0.25 percentage points off the APR when enrolled in autopay.

## 3. Fees
- Origination fee: 1% to 5% of the loan amount, deducted from the proceeds before funding.
- Late fee: the greater of $15 or 5% of the overdue payment, charged after a 10-day grace period.
- Returned payment fee: $30.
- There is no prepayment penalty.

## 4. Funding
Funds are sent 1 to 3 business days after final approval. You may cancel within 3 business days of funding at no cost by returning the full amount.

## 5. Permitted Use
Loan proceeds may not be used for business purposes, post-secondary tuition, or gambling.

## 6. Hardship Deferral
A borrower facing financial hardship may request a payment deferral of up to 3 months. This is allowed once during the life of the loan. Interest continues to accrue during the deferral.

## 7. Delinquency
- Accounts 30 days past due are reported to credit bureaus.
- Accounts 60 days past due may be referred to collections.
