# Harborview Customer Servicing FAQ

## Contact
- Phone: 1-800-555-0142
- Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time.
- Closed on weekends and federal holidays.

## Beneficiary Changes
Beneficiary changes must be made using the signed Beneficiary Change Form. Phone requests are not accepted. Changes take effect on the date Harborview receives the completed form.

## Address and Contact Updates
You may update your mailing address online through your account portal or by phone after identity verification.

## Complaints
Submit complaints in writing or by phone. Harborview acknowledges complaints within 5 business days and provides a written response within 30 days.

## Statements
Annuity statements are issued annually. Loan statements are issued monthly.

## What This Assistant Cannot Do
This assistant answers questions about Harborview annuity and personal loan policies only. It does not provide investment, tax, or legal advice, and it cannot access or change individual customer accounts.
